Segmented Home Network (VLANs + pfSense)

Segmenting my home network into five VLANs on a pfSense firewall, a Netgear GS308EP managed switch and a TP-Link EAP610 access point: Management, Trusted, IoT, Guest and Servers, each on its own /24 (10.0.VLAN.0). The switch trunks tagged VLANs to pfSense and the AP; each SSID maps to its own VLAN, so IoT gear and guests never share a broadcast domain with my own devices. Inter-VLAN traffic is default-deny, with written exceptions such as "every VLAN may reach Pi-hole on port 53". The write-up covers the design, the port map, and the problems I hit on the way.

Pi-hole Network DNS

A Raspberry Pi running Pi-hole as the DNS server for my home network, filtering ads and trackers for every device. When the Pi lost its static address after a reboot and DNS stopped resolving, I found it with an nmap ping sweep, pinned its address, and wrote up the fix. After the VLAN build, Pi-hole serves DNS to every VLAN from the Servers segment.
